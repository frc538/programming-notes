# Autonomous & Kickoff Prep

## Summary

- Revised 2 pathplanner implementations and 1 autopilot implementation to be compatible with the classroom and validated that they appeared to work as advertised.
- Discussed software team expectations for the kickoff and things to come after.

## Autonomous

### Pathplanner

We successfully loaded pathplanner projects developed by Carl and Owen onto the 2025 chassis and tested them out.  We learned as part of this exercise that pathplanner projects compile to data files that are directly loaded into the project.

Pathplanner can be used to generate on-the-fly paths also, though this was not explored at this time.

### Autopilot

We lowered the autopilot max velocity and max acceleration constraints to better suit first execution in a classroom.  The autopilot implementation separated the initial position of the robot from the desired target location, providing 3 sample initial positions.  This was loaded on the robot and executed with a 0.5 m/s max velocity, and then again with 1 m/s max velocity with ok results.  One of the runs ended with the robot not within tolerance of the target position which left it "shivering" due to being stuck in a decel phase.  

Autopilot is a simple implementation that depends on tuning of the underlying drive base to get the best performance.  Tuning of the drive base and also of the autopilot constraints is necessary to ensure adequate performance.

We learned some of the math underlying autopilot algorithm.
