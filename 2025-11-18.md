## Philosophy of auto & teleop control level

I described my objective of the next couple years in terms of how we approach autonomous and teleop as raising the level of control.  In prior years we would have a driver and mechanism operator.  Their control of individual aspects of the robot is completely separate and requires coordination of actions in order to accomplish complex tasks.

2025 Game Example:
- Driver - Approach the reef
- Mechanism - Raise the elevator to the appropriate level for the ball
- Mechanism - Aim the arm towards the ball, tweaking it as
- Driver - Slowly maneuvers into the reef, after capture backing up
- Mechanism - Lower the elevator for driving
- Driver - Then drive ...

You'll notice in the example that this method of control requires constant coordination between the Driver and Mechanism controller that results in either slow execution or errors in execution.

Raising the level of control is about:
- Decomposing the necessary actions into high level commands
  - Go to point
  - Get the ball from the middle level and secure for travel
  - Go to the barge
  - Score in the barge
- Changing jobs of the operators
  - Pilot - In charge of driving and mechanism triggers
  - Navigator - Configures navigation waypoints, targets, queuing operations, offloads pilot as required

Autonomous routines should build on automatic capability implemented for teleop.  If teleop operations are consistently successful, then the autonomous routine can be constructed from the high level commands like building-blocks.

## Initial robot position

The robot is not guaranteed to be in a consistent starting position from run to run.  The load-in team will do their best to assure good placement, but fields have tolerance, and load-in teams have tolerance.  Additionally, unless the various starting positions have known good visibility to limelight tags, you may not be able to depend on limelight for initial localization.

The method discussed in the session used these steps:
- Create a StartPositionChooser with various starting points pre-defined
- Create a method in RobotContainer to set the drive pose based on the StartPositionChooser
- Call the method from Robot.java in the DisabledPeriodic function
