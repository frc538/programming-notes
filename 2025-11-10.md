# Limelight Configuration and AdvantageKit Vision Template Integration

## Summary

In this session, we discussed the various uses of cameras in FRC robotics, including:
- Navigation (robot localization)
- Telemetry (display on the driver station)
- Target tracking / orientation

We reviewed the [limelight configuration](https://docs.limelightvision.io/docs/docs-limelight/pipeline-apriltag/apriltags) for bacon and eggs, the two cameras on the 2025 bot.  The calibration provided by the quick start provides a reasonable starting point, but may require variation due to lighting conditions in the school and on each playing field.  The lighting in any of the arenas is guaranteed to be very different from that of our robotics rooms.

After walking through camera calibration, we configured the [AdvantageKit Vision Template](https://docs.advantagekit.org/getting-started/template-projects/vision-template/) with the bacon and eggs configured cameras and confirmed good detection of limelight tags.

With the limelights operational, we integrated the vision subsystem into the drivebase project that we developed last week, giving us a functional drive base code with vision providing pose estimates.  At this point we tested the robot slowly rotating with a single tag visible and noticed an artifact as the tag comes into view, driving discussion into filtering.

The vision template provides some simplistic filtering that forces rejection of pose estimates based on a variety of criteria such as:
- Checking for at least one tag observed
- Checking for a maximum value of ambiguity (a measurement provided by the limelight)
- Checking for reasonable X, Y, or Z

Once the position estimate is accepted, it is incorporated with a standard deviation 

Future tuning will be required to turn this generally ok solution into one that is trustworthy in real game environments.
